Machine

TargzPenPlotterCtrl as hardware. Everything is wired to everything: jog and the readout moves, trip a limit and the run block dies.

01 / Option A · jog by keys

Keys are discrete: one press, one step, picked in advance. The readout sits above because a jog control is only judgeable next to the numbers it moves. A coordinate is a place, so the strip under it is a travel with a pip on it, not a fill.

discrete · one press, one step

02 / Option B · jog by stick

A stick is continuous: a direction, an amount, and it keeps going while you hold it. Everything below the XY control is identical, so the only difference is the one being argued about. Z stays on keys.

continuous · direction and amount, while you hold it

03 / The pen bank · a key that carries its own height

Today it is two objects for one fact: a key that sends the pen to a height, and a pencil that opens a popover to change it. Here the height is a window in the key’s own face. The knob turns it, double-click types it, and it notches at the default on the way past.

3 heights · window in the cap · notch at the default

04 / The overrides · a percentage is a quantity, so it is a knob

Five controls per value in the app, three times over. A quantity is a knob; only a length keeps a track, and 100% is not a length. The notch at 100 is what RESET was for. RESET stays anyway: no knob is fast at slamming back mid-job.

1–500% · notch at 100 · shift = fine

05 / Run · and the one that hurts goes under a cover

ABORT sits with START and STOP, same size, different tint. Colour is not a guard. A hinged cover is: two deliberate actions, and it springs shut after six seconds. The mushroom latches.

flip the cover · the mushroom latches · twist to release

06 / The annunciator · which one tripped

One red bar with a sentence in it is not an alarm panel. A machine has a dozen inputs and the question when it halts is which. Every legend is engraved and dark, so you learn the panel before you need it.

8 legends · dark by default · UNLOCK acknowledges

07 / The job meter · segments, because a fill cannot tick

At 2am the question is whether it is still moving, and a fill creeping 0.3% a minute looks exactly like a fill that stopped. Segments tick. The percentage keeps its own glass, because segments are for watching and a number is for reporting.

40 segments · the head one is hot · 4 engraved fields

08 / The tape · the console as a roll, not a text area

The log comes up from the bottom, newest at the tear edge, older lines darker because they are further into the machine. It is the only place here where colour is data. Open the port, then type into the slot and press SEND.

newest at the tear edge · colour is the direction

09 / The status rail · the chassis, and it is not a control

The app’s status bar wears the same borders and hovers as its toolbars, so it reads as a row of things you might click. None of them do anything. Milled into the case instead.

read-only by construction

10 / The bill

What the second document changes about the argument.

Buys

  • The language survived a second app, mostly for free. Fourteen of twenty-two controls needed no new part. That is the only real test of a design language.
  • A guarded key is a safety property, not a style. ABORT and DELETE LAYER are the two irreversible things in targz and both are a red tint today. A cover cannot be hit by accident, and it is eleven lines of CSS.
  • The annunciator is legible before anything is wrong. You learn the panel while it is dark.
  • Here, skeuomorphism is not a costume. The plotter has a physical e-stop, physical limit switches and a physical carriage.
  • The dark chassis stops being a problem. A control app's job is a machine, not a drawing, and it is looked at from across a room, late.

Costs

  • A second copy of the kit. physical-ui-kit.js and the inline copy in the layers document will not stay identical. One has to become the other's import.
  • The real app is Metro4 and jQuery. This is its anatomy rebuilt, not a re-skin, so adopting any of it means replacing the widget underneath first.
  • Space. One DRO is bigger than the app's whole position strip.
  • It has to survive being wrong. A bad hatch wastes paper. A mis-reported ALARM costs a plot, a pen, or a gantry.
  • The mushroom is a lie unless it is wired. If it does not stop the machine, it must not look like the thing that does.
